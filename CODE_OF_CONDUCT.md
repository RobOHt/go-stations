# Code of conduct

GO Stations is built in the open for people who ride GO trains. Everyone taking part
in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to help keep it a
welcoming place.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to iOS.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific, and useful.
- Respect people's privacy: never share someone's personal details, and don't ask
  for them.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and
block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem, open an issue titled
"Conduct contact" with no details, and the maintainer will get in touch privately.
